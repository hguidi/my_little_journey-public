# Asset Provenance

## Project

**My Little Journey**

This document records the provenance and licensing status of the creative
assets distributed with the project.

## Ownership declaration

All original artwork, sprites, icons, visual effects, and music created
specifically for My Little Journey were generated using artificial intelligence
tools under terms that grant the project owner the necessary rights to use the
generated output, including commercial use and distribution as part of the
game.

The generated assets may have been edited, resized, animated, converted, or
otherwise adapted for use in the game. Those adaptations were made for My
Little Journey and remain part of the project's asset collection.

No project-specific artwork or music is knowingly copied from, or intended to
represent, a third-party character, franchise, logo, musical recording, artist,
or other protected brand.

## Covered assets

This declaration covers the project-specific creative content under the
following directories:

- `assets/sprites/`
- `assets/audio/`
- `assets/icons/`

It also covers project-specific images stored elsewhere in the repository,
including branding and store-listing artwork.

## Internal evidence to retain

The project owner should privately retain the following records when they are
available. They do not need to be committed to this public repository:

- Names of the AI services or models used
- Account or subscription tier used during generation
- Approximate generation dates
- Copies or archived links to the applicable commercial-use terms
- Original generated files and prompts, when retained
- Receipts or subscription records, when applicable

Do not commit account credentials, private prompts, receipts, API keys, or other
personal information to this repository.

## Third-party software

This declaration applies to project-specific creative assets only. It does not
replace the licenses of Godot Engine, Android tooling, or any other third-party
software used to build or distribute the game. Those components remain subject
to their respective licenses and notice requirements.

## Review before release

Before each public release, the project owner should confirm that:

- Newly added creative assets have the same commercial-use rights.
- No asset intentionally imitates protected third-party material.
- Any asset obtained from a different source is documented separately.
- Required engine and third-party software notices are included.

# Privacy Policy for My Little Journey

**Effective date:** July 27, 2026  
**Developer:** Henrique Guidi  
**Contact:** henrique.guidi@gmail.com

My Little Journey is a game designed for young children. Protecting children
and their privacy is important to us. The game is designed to work offline and
does not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share personal information through
the game. This policy applies to the Android version of My Little Journey,
package identifier `com.mylittlejourney.game`, distributed by Henrique Guidi.

## Information we collect

My Little Journey does not collect personal information or other user data.
The game does not collect names, email addresses, account information,
locations, device identifiers, advertising identifiers, gameplay analytics,
photos, audio recordings, contacts, or other personal or sensitive data.

## Accounts and sign-in

The game does not require or provide user accounts, registration, or sign-in.

## Internet access and third-party services

The game does not require an internet connection and does not use advertising,
analytics, social networking, authentication, or other online third-party
services. It does not contain external links accessible through normal
gameplay.

## Platform services

Google Play, Android, and the device manufacturer may independently process
information related to downloading or installing the game, operating the
device, crash reporting, or store usage under their own privacy policies. My
Little Journey does not control those platform services and does not transmit
information to them from within the game.

## Advertising and purchases

The game does not display advertising and does not contain in-app purchases,
paid promotions, or personalized commercial content.

## Children's privacy

My Little Journey is intended for young children, including children under 13.
Because the game does not collect or transmit user data, it does not knowingly
collect personal information from children. If you believe that information
has been provided to us in connection with the game, a parent or guardian may
contact the developer so that we can investigate and take appropriate action.

## Information stored on the device

The game uses temporary in-memory state to operate the current play session. It
does not create user profiles or intentionally save gameplay progress or
personal information. Session state is cleared when the game is restarted or
closed and is not transmitted to the developer or any third party.

## Data sharing and retention

Because the game does not collect user data through the app, no in-game user
data is shared, sold, or retained by the developer. Email correspondence is
handled separately as described below.

## Contact communications

If a parent or guardian contacts the developer by email, the developer receives
the sender's email address and message. This information is used only to respond
to the inquiry, is not sold or used for advertising, and is retained only as
reasonably necessary to handle the request or comply with law. The sender may
request deletion of the correspondence.

## Security

The game is designed to minimize privacy risk by operating offline and avoiding
the collection of personal information. No method of software distribution is
completely free from security risk, but the game does not maintain a server or
remote user database containing player information.

## Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if the game's features or legal requirements change.
Any updated policy will include a revised effective date. If a future version
introduces data collection or online services, this policy and the applicable
store disclosures will be updated before that version is released.
